FAQs
Frequently Asked Questions

How often should windows be professionally cleaned?

Most homes and businesses benefit from professional window cleaning every 3–6 months, depending on location, weather conditions, and the amount of dirt or pollen buildup. Commercial properties or high-traffic areas may require more frequent cleanings to maintain a clear, professional appearance year-round.

Do you clean both interior and exterior windows?

Yes, we provide both interior and exterior window cleaning services. Our team carefully cleans glass, frames, and window areas to help improve visibility, appearance, and natural light for your home or business.

Will window cleaning remove hard water stains?

Professional window cleaning can help reduce or remove many hard water stains, depending on the severity of the buildup. In some cases, specialized products or restoration techniques may be needed for tougher stains. Our team will assess the condition of your windows and recommend the best cleaning solution to improve their clarity and appearance.
