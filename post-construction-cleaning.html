Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can cleaning start after construction is completed?

Cleaning can typically begin as soon as construction or renovation work is finished and the area is safe to access. We recommend scheduling your post-construction cleaning shortly after the project is completed so dust, debris, and residue can be removed quickly, leaving the space clean, polished, and ready to use.

Do you handle all types of construction debris removal?

We handle the cleanup of common post-construction dust, dirt, and light debris left behind after renovation or building projects. However, for safety and disposal regulations, certain heavy materials or hazardous waste may require a specialized removal service. Let us know the scope of your project, and we’ll be happy to discuss the best cleanup solution for your needs.

Is post-construction cleaning suitable for all surfaces?

Yes, our post-construction cleaning services are designed to safely clean a variety of surfaces, including floors, countertops, windows, fixtures, and more. We use appropriate cleaning methods and products for different materials to help protect your newly finished space while removing dust, residue, and debris left behind after construction.
