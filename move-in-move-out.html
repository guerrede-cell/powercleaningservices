Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book move in/move out cleaning?

We recommend booking your move-in or move-out cleaning at least 1–2 weeks in advance to ensure availability, especially during busy moving seasons. However, we understand that moving schedules can change quickly, and we will always do our best to accommodate last-minute appointments whenever possible.

Do you clean appliances as part of the service?

Yes, we can clean appliances as part of our cleaning services. This may include wiping down the exterior and interior of appliances such as refrigerators, ovens, microwaves, and more. If you would like appliance deep cleaning included, please let us know when scheduling your appointment so we can customize the service to your needs.

Is there a difference in the cleaning process for move in vs. move out?

Yes, there are slight differences between move-in and move-out cleaning services. Move-out cleaning focuses on thoroughly cleaning and preparing the property for the next occupant, including removing dust, dirt, and buildup left behind. Move-in cleaning is designed to sanitize and refresh the space before you settle in, helping ensure your new home or business feels clean, safe, and ready to enjoy.
