Frequently Asked Questions

Does someone need to be present?

No, you do not need to be present during the cleaning service as long as we have safe access to your home or business. Many of our clients provide a key, door code, or special instructions for entry. Our professional cleaning team is trustworthy, reliable, and committed to respecting your property while delivering high-quality cleaning services. If you prefer to be present, that is completely fine too!

Is Power Cleaning insured?

Yes, Power Cleaning is fully insured and committed to providing safe, reliable, and professional cleaning services. Our insurance coverage helps protect your home or business while giving you peace of mind during every cleaning appointment. We take pride in delivering dependable service you can trust.

Is Power Cleaning pet safe?

Yes, Power Cleaning uses cleaning products and methods that are safe for pets when used properly. We understand that pets are part of the family, and our team takes extra care to create a clean and healthy environment for both you and your furry friends. If you have specific concerns about products or sensitivities, let us know before your appointment so we can accommodate your needs.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No, you do not need to provide cleaning supplies. Our team arrives fully equipped with professional-grade cleaning products and equipment to complete the job efficiently. If you prefer that we use specific products in your home or business, we’re happy to accommodate your request.

What if I do not have a mop, bucket, or vacuum?

No problem! Our cleaning team brings all the necessary cleaning equipment and supplies, including mops, buckets, vacuums, and professional cleaning products. You can count on us to arrive fully prepared and ready to make your home or business spotless.

What if I just had renovation work done?

We offer post-construction and post-renovation cleaning services to help remove dust, debris, and leftover materials after your project is complete. Our team will thoroughly clean your space so you can enjoy your newly renovated home or business without the mess.

Can I provide special instructions or make specific requests for my cleaning?

Absolutely! We encourage clients to share any special instructions, priorities, or specific cleaning requests before their appointment. Whether you want extra attention on certain areas, prefer specific cleaning products, or have special access instructions, our team will do our best to customize the service to fit your needs.
